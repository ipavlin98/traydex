Traydex portable

While this file sits next to Traydex.exe, Traydex keeps the shelf, notes, settings and logs
in the "data" folder here instead of %LOCALAPPDATA%\Traydex. Move the whole folder to take
everything with you. Delete this file to switch back to the usual location.

Start with Windows and the Explorer menu item are off until you turn them on in Settings;
they point to this copy of Traydex.exe.
